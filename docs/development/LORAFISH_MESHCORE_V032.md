# LoRaFish MeshCore Edition v0.3.2

- Restores feeding interaction on the aquarium.
- Long-press ENTER while viewing the aquarium starts a food pellet drop.
- Fish swims toward the falling pellet.
- Pellet disappears when eaten.
- One short buzzer pip is played through MeshCore's existing genericBuzzer API.
- Feeding adds +3 happiness, capped at 100, and persists the pet state.
- Buzzer mute preference is respected.
- Clock-page time moved upward by 5 pixels to separate it from the date.
- v0.3.1 fish sprite, BLE pairing, Companion protocol and radio behaviour are unchanged.
